# Chapter 2: readiness tracker

`readiness-tracker.csv` is the 30-row table from Chapter 2, blank. Open it in any spreadsheet and rate each task statement 1 to 5 in `rating_ch02`. Re-rate a domain's rows in `rating_after_practice` after its practice set, and once more in `rating_after_mock`.

Any row under 4 gets a dated session in your Chapter 12 plan.
